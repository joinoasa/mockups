Legged robots for extreme terrain

Two open legged platforms for ground that tracks can't cross: a 4-legged dog for speed and carrying, and a 6-legged spider that always keeps three feet down. Both share the payload rails, radio and e-stop used on G-10. Block shapes only; sizes are rough guesses.
