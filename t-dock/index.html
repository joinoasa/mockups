T-DOCK drone docks

Docks that let drones fly, land and recharge with no one there. Precision landing (T-DOCK1), auto charging to the OASA-DOCK standard (T-DOCK2) and landing on a moving deck (T-DOCK3), shown in four forms. Block shapes only; sizes are rough guesses.
