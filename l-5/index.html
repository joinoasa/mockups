L-5 solar blimps

Lighter-than-air platforms that hang over an area for days or weeks as a high radio relay and an eye in the sky. One envelope, gondola, solar skin and radio, from a tethered balloon to a drone-carrying mothership. Block shapes only; sizes are rough guesses.
