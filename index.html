OASA 3D mockups

Rough, block-level concepts of nodes on the OASA tech tree. Rotate them, take them apart and tap any part to see what it does. They're for talking ideas through, not designs to build from.

R-COM5

Open SDR radio

One shared radio core with plug-in band cards, in field brick, stackable, handheld, backpack, vehicle, pole and drone versions.

Open → G-10

Open tracked platform

The tracked chassis other ground robots build on: landscaping, snow, trash pickup, drone carrier and underground types, with eight quick-attach tools.

Open → G-2

Legged robots for extreme terrain

A 4-legged dog and a 6-legged spider sharing G-10's payloads, radio and e-stop, with their walking gaits.

Open → T-SITE1

Survey drone

Quad, fixed-wing and VTOL survey drones with swappable sensors, flying a mapping grid over a site for the landscaping robot.

Open → T-DOCK

Drone docks

Field dock, battery swap dock, mobile hive trailer and a wave-stabilized buoy dock, with the full launch, landing and charging cycle.

Open → S-3

Solar boat

Solar catamaran, trimaran and sail-assisted hulls from 1 m to 12 m, rebuilt as a survey, water sampling, cleanup, drone carrier and sub gateway boat, with see-through water to watch what happens below.

Open → T-NODE2

Mesh node kits

Tower, vehicle, home and backpack kits built on one node core, plus a mesh view with live links and best-path routing across a valley.
